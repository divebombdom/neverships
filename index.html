Questions

Is this a real store?

No. NeverShips is a parody. No products exist, no payments are taken, and nothing is shipped. All brand and product names are fictional.

Will I be charged?

Never. The checkout has no real payment fields. It only uses a pre-filled fake card and sends nothing anywhere.

Where does my data go?

Your cart, orders, and stats stay in a small cookie in your browser. Clear your browser data and they disappear, just like your package. See the privacy policy for details about ads and cookies.
