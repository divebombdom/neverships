About

The store where nothing ships.

NeverShips is a parody of online tech shopping. You get the rush of filling a cart with phones, graphics cards, and gadgets — the browsing, the deals, the checkout, the tracking — without spending a cent or receiving a thing.

It's inspired by "dopamine sites" like FoodNeverComes, which let you order food that never arrives. We applied the same idea to the most impulse-buy-prone category on the internet: tech.

What's real here

  • The shopping experience: carts, options, deals, checkout, tracking.
  • The ads, which are clearly labeled and help keep the site free.
  • The shopping guides, which are genuinely useful.

What isn't

  • The products, brands, prices, reviews, drivers, and warehouses. All fictional.
  • Payments. There are no payment fields and no money ever changes hands.
  • Shipping. Obviously.

NeverShips is not affiliated with any real retailer, manufacturer, or carrier. Product names are parodies and fictional.
