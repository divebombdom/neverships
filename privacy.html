What we store

NeverShips has no accounts and no backend database. Your cart, order history, and stats are saved in a small first-party cookie in your own browser. It contains no personal information and is never used for advertising. You can erase them at any time using "Reset my stats" in the footer or by clearing your browser data.

We never collect payment details. The checkout uses a fixed demonstration card.
