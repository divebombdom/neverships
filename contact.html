Get in touch

NeverShips is a small, independent parody site. We read every message, though we can't promise a reply as fast as our drivers promise a delivery.

Before you write

  • Did you place an order? It isn't coming. Nothing on NeverShips is for sale, no payment was taken, and nothing will ship. See our About page.
  • Need help with a real order from another store? Contact that store directly. Our guide to missing packages explains what to do.

How to reach us

Email us at ratgalactic@gmail.com

Write to us about:

  • Corrections or suggestions for our shopping guides.
  • Bug reports and accessibility problems.
  • Ideas for new fake products or real guides.
  • Privacy questions, press, and partnership inquiries.

Please don't send payment details or other sensitive information. You can also report bugs publicly on our GitHub project page.
